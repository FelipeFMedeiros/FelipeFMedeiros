# Icon sources

Technology logos are vendored from Devicon, Simple Icons and the official Elysia documentation. The pinned source URLs and repository revisions are recorded in `sources.json`; available upstream license texts are in `licenses/`.

- Devicon: https://github.com/devicons/devicon (MIT).
- Simple Icons: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0).
- Elysia artwork: https://github.com/elysiajs/documentation.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Selected monochrome logos are recolored for legibility on the dark badges. Portfolio and email pictograms are drawn locally in the generator.
